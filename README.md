# Hey, I'm Kat.

I'm a product designer who's spent the better part of two decades working with technology, and I'm still fascinated by how people make sense of it.

My interests bounce around design, psychology, human behaviour and the social sciences. I'm particularly interested in the assumptions we make when designing technology, the people those assumptions leave out, and why we keep making some of the same mistakes.

You'll find me asking *why*, especially when the answer seems to be *because that's how we've always done it*.

## What I'm tinkering with

**[gestalt.fyi](https://gestalt.fyi)**

Lately, I've been pouring more time into understanding and learning frontend development. As a designer I've spent my whole working life collaborating with engineers, so I have a decent idea of the limitations and capabilities (or so I thought). Now I want to really get my hands dirty.

gestalt.fyi is just what will eventually become a place for thoughts, ideas, and experiments. Currently in progress is an offshoot - a **[field guide](https://gestalt.fyi/field-guide/)** to the ideas, behaviours, principles and practices that influence how we design and use technology.

I'm building the site with Astro, and am learning as I go in a really chaotic trial and error 'process'.
